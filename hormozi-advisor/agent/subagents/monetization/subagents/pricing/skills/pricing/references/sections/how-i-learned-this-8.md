## How I Learned This

I learned this from a tailor. I was getting fitted for a suit. The first one he had me try on was $16,000. I almost let out an audible gasp when he told me the price. He saw my reaction. He quickly gave me a $2000 suit to try on. When I looked at the tag, I let out a sigh of relief. What made it so impactful for me was that I had told myself I’d spend no more than $500. So, it worked.
