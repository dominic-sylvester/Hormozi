## How It Works

Price anchors *anchor* the price in the prospect’s mind. I don’t know what the psychology is behind it. And frankly, I don’t care that much. I just know that it works. All you have to do is add something to your suite of products or services that’s 10x or more expensive than your core offer. Think of it like the ‘mac daddy’ version. To be clear, you can offer whatever you want. Just make sure that if someone buys it, you’re excited they did.
