## Steps To Implement It

To make this happen. Think of the most absurd version of your thing. Think of the price you’d happily do it for. Now, start offering that first. After all, if it is the mac daddy, it’s probably the *best thing for them* if they can afford it. So, get the gasp. And if they can’t, act like the tailor and find something better suited for them *your main offer*.

As a fun side note, a close friend of mine implemented this in his business, and almost overnight, everyone started buying his mac daddy product. Now, it’s all he sells. And that change made him more money to the tune of *millions per year*. Worth it.
