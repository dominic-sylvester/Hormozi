Answer: an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. My second book, *$100M Leads*, answered the next natural question: *How do I find these people?*  
Answer: You advertise. This book, *$100M Money Models*, answers the next natural question: *How do I get them to buy it?*  
Answer: A Money Model.
