## 2. Always Get A Credit Card

| But Why Do You Need My Card? | *Shrug*... That's just how we've always done it... |

**Always Get A Credit Card.** Record their info, hold onto the ID, and motion for their credit card saying, _“What card do you wanna use?”_ They have to leave a card. If they balk, just say _“That’s just how we’ve always done it.”_ If they still refuse, wish them a lovely day and show them out.

> **Pro Tip:** If someone doesn’t agree to put their card down *and* do the work, I won’t sell them. They complain more and they convert less. Not worth the hassle.
