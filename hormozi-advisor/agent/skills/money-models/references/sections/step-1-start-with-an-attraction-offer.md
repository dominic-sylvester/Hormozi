## Step 1) Start With An Attraction Offer.
The goal is to turn strangers into customers and cover our costs. So, figure out what you’re going to sell. Then, figure out the best way to present it. The Attraction Offer section has my top favorites: Win Your Money Back, Giveaways, Decoy Offers, Buy X Get Y Free, Pay Less Now or Pay More Later. Then, advertise it. If you get leads who turn into customers, you’re on your way. Figuring out what works best may take up to a year.
