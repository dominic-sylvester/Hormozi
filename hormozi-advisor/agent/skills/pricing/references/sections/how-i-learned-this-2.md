## How I Learned This

Let’s talk about adding sales tax to your pricing. This isn’t just about following the law - it’s about protecting your profits.

I learned this from a Canadian marketer charging me $25,000 a month. I agreed. But when I saw the invoice, it said $27,000 per month. His province had 8% sales tax. It was there clear as day:  
$$25,000 \times 1.08 = 27,000$$  
When I asked him about it he said, “I don’t cover taxes. My price is what I get.” I just took note and paid the invoice. Blunt, but effective.
