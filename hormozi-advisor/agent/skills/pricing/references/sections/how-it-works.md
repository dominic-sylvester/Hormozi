## How It Works

Monthly billing gives you 12 cycles a year. Weekly (or every 28 days) gives you 13. That extra cycle? Pure profit. And the best part, people convert at the same rates. So this gives you an instant and permanent 8.3% increase in revenue for no extra cost. So if you had a 20% net margin business, implementing this would take you from 20% to 26.1% net margins and would increase your net profit by a whopping 41.5%.
